% Research statement
\begin{statementbox}
\noindent I am an ex-student researcher at \textbf{Google DeepMind} and \textbf{Microsoft Research}.
I study how individuals come together to form shared \textbf{values}, \textbf{cognition},
and \textbf{culture}, and what changes when \textbf{AI systems} join this process. My work asks how AI differs
from \textbf{human intelligence}; what large language models, as compressed \textbf{collective human knowledge},
reveal about \textbf{human behavior and society}; and how AI, as a \textbf{social actor}, reshapes
\textbf{social dynamics} and \textbf{power structures}.
\end{statementbox}
